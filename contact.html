Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our bespoke tailoring process, delivery, and services.

1. How long does custom tailoring take?
Typically, our custom tailored garments take 7 to 14 business days from your initial measurement and fabric confirmation to completion. For expedited requests (weddings, red carpet events, or urgent travel), express crafting within 3–5 days can be arranged upon consultation.
2. Do you offer delivery across Nigeria and internationally?
Yes, we provide secure doorstep delivery across Lagos and ship nationwide via trusted courier partners (including Abuja, Port Harcourt, and Ibadan). We also cater to our international diaspora clientele across the UK, USA, Canada, and Europe via DHL Express with complete tracking.
3. Can I bring my own fabric?
Absolutely! We welcome clients who bring their personal fabrics, whether heirloom aso-oke, imported Irish linen, English cashmere, or vintage brocade. Our master cutters will assess the drape and texture to recommend the optimal silhouette and cut for your chosen textile.
4. What is your alteration and fitting policy?
Perfection is our promise. We offer complimentary precision alterations on all bespoke creations within 30 days of garment delivery to ensure your fit is second to none. Simply notify us and schedule a fitting appointment at our Lekki showroom.
