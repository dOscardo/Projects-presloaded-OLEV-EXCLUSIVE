Our Heritage & Origin

Rooted in Tradition, Tailored for Royalty

Founded in Lagos with a passion for perfection, Olev Exclusive was born from a deep appreciation for Nigerian sartorial excellence. Our journey began with a simple belief — that every person deserves to wear garments that reflect their dignity, culture, and unique style. Today, we stand as a beacon of premium African tailoring, blending time-honoured Nigerian craftsmanship with contemporary design sensibilities.

From our flagship studio in Lekki Phase 1, we cater to distinguished men and women who regard their attire as an extension of their personal stature. Each bespoke piece is conceptualized, hand-cut, and meticulously finished to elevate the wearer's poise in any room across the globe.

Our Craftsmanship Process

Five disciplined milestones from raw textile to an exquisite garment worthy of perfection.

1
Step 01

Fabric Selection

We source the finest fabrics from across Nigeria and the world — guinea brocade, aso-oke, Italian wool, premium ankara prints.

2
Step 02

Measurement & Consultation

Precise body measurements combined with understanding your style preferences and the occasion.

3
Step 03

Pattern & Cutting

Expert pattern-making and precision cutting by our master craftsmen.

4
Step 04

Stitching & Assembly

Meticulous hand and machine stitching, with attention to every seam, hem, and detail.

5
Step 05

Finishing & Quality Check

Final pressing, quality inspection, and the perfect finishing touches before delivery.
